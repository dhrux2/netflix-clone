## Expanded catalog and stock previews

The catalog contains 36 original fictional titles: 17 shows and 19 movies. The two Top 10 rows are editorial demo selections, not real popularity rankings.

Pexels stock footage is shown in hover previews and playable in title details for matching ocean/city titles. Full demo playback remains the separately credited Big Buck Bunny trailer. Stock previews do not create watch-progress checkpoints. Autoplay is muted, disabled for reduced-motion users, paused in hidden tabs, and falls back to artwork on video errors.

Sources (checked 2026-10-26):
- Mikhail Nilov, ocean waves: https://www.pexels.com/video/a-drone-footage-of-ocean-waves-6981292/
- TripAway 2Day, city at night: https://www.pexels.com/video/a-city-at-night-6345160/

Remote MP4 links are centralized in src/data/preview-media.ts. They can change; for deployment, use licensed assets hosted on storage you control or refresh links through the official Pexels API. No API key is required for these two selected direct links. Unsplash supplies still imagery, not these video previews.

Fallback update: titles without a selected Pexels clip now use the labeled Big Buck Bunny demo trailer in the expanded card as well. They do not imply a real trailer for the fictional title.
